Daily Math Puzzle 2026/11/01

Daily Math Puzzle on X

English

Let \(n\) be an integer with \(n\geq2\). We want to trace every line segment of a grid with \(n\) rows and \(n\) columns of cells exactly once. The pen may be put down or lifted only at grid points. At every grid point encountered within a stroke, the pen must turn right; it may neither go straight nor turn back. What is the minimum number of strokes needed?

日本語

\(n\) を2以上の整数とする． 縦横 \(n\) マスの方眼の線を，すべて1回ずつなぞりたい． ペンを下ろす点と上げる点は格子点に限る． 途中の格子点では必ず右に曲がり，直進も引き返しもできない． 最少何筆でなぞれるか．
