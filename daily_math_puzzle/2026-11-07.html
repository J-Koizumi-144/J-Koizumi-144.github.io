Daily Math Puzzle 2026/11/07

Daily Math Puzzle on X

English

A group of \(n\geq3\) people takes some photographs. Each photograph shows at least two people, but none shows all \(n\) people. Every pair of people must appear together in exactly one photograph. What is the minimum number of photographs needed?

日本語

\(n\geq3\) 人で，何枚かの写真を撮る． 各写真には2人以上が写り，全員が一緒に写る写真は撮らない． どの2人についても，一緒に写っている写真がちょうど1枚になるようにしたい． 写真は最少で何枚必要か．
