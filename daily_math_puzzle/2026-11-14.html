Daily Math Puzzle 2026/11/14

Daily Math Puzzle on X

English

Draw one or more nonintersecting semicircles above a line, with all their endpoints equally spaced along the line. Reflect a copy of the drawing across the line, then slide the copy one space to the right. Prove that if you start at the leftmost point and follow the arcs without turning back, you visit every point and finish at the rightmost point.

日本語

直線の上側に，互いに交わらない半円を何本か描く．すべての半円の両端は，この直線上に等間隔に並んでいる．図の写しを上下に裏返し，点一つ分だけ右にずらして重ねる．左端の点から半円を引き返さずにたどると，すべての点を通って右端の点に着くことを示せ．
