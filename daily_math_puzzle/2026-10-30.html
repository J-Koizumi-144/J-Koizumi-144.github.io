Daily Math Puzzle 2026/10/30

Daily Math Puzzle on X

English

There are \(n\) cups, each containing the same mass of salt water, where \(n\geq2\). Initially, the concentration in each cup is either 6% or 12%. You may repeatedly choose two cups, mix all their contents, and divide the mixture equally between those two cups. On at most one occasion, you may choose three cups instead of two and perform the same operation. Find all \(n\) for which, regardless of how the initial concentrations are assigned, all cups can be made to have the same concentration after finitely many operations.

日本語

\(n\) 個のコップに，同じ重さの食塩水が入っている（\(n\geq2\)）． 各コップの初めの濃度は6%または12%である． 2個のコップを選び，中身をすべて混ぜてから，その2個に等分して戻す操作を繰り返せる． ただし，一度だけ，2個の代わりに3個を選んで同じ操作をしてもよい． 初めの濃度の割り当てがどのようであっても，有限回の操作ですべてのコップの濃度を等しくできる \(n\) をすべて求めよ．
