Daily Math Puzzle 2026/10/27

Original Post on X

English

Fourteen people sit around a circular table. Neighbors are always friends, and each person has an odd number of friends among these fourteen people. Also, every group of three people contains a pair of friends. Find the minimum possible number of pairs of friends. Friendship is mutual, and no one is counted as their own friend.

日本語

14人が円卓を囲んでいる． 隣同士は必ず友人であり，どの人もこの14人の中に奇数人の友人がいる． また，どの3人を選んでも，その中に友人同士の2人がいる． 友人同士の2人組の個数の最小値を求めよ． ただし，友人関係は相互的で，自分自身は友人に数えない．
