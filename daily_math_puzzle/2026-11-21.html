Daily Math Puzzle 2026/11/21

Original Post on X

English

Mark some points inside triangle \(ABC\) and triangulate it using exactly those points and \(A,B,C\) as vertices. Color each edge except the three outer sides red, blue, or green. Prove that there is a coloring in which every marked point can reach \(A\) along red edges alone, \(B\) along blue edges alone, and \(C\) along green edges alone.

日本語

三角形 \(ABC\) の内部にいくつかの点を打ち，それらと \(A,B,C\) を頂点として三角形分割する． 外周の3辺を除く各辺を，赤・青・緑のいずれか1色に塗る． 打ったどの点からも，赤い辺だけで \(A\) に，青い辺だけで \(B\) に，緑の辺だけで \(C\) に行ける塗り方があることを示せ．
