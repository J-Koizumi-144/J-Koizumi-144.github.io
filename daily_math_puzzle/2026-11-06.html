Daily Math Puzzle 2026/11/06

Original Post on X

English

Nine people are seated around a circular table. They hold distinct numbers of coins, from one to nine. In each round, everyone may simultaneously give at most one coin to each of their two neighbors. Only coins held at the start of a round may be given away in that round. Prove that, regardless of the seating arrangement, it is possible to make everyone hold five coins each in five rounds.

日本語

9人が円卓を囲んでいる． 持っているコインの枚数は全員異なり，1枚から9枚までである． 1回の操作では，全員が同時に，左右の隣の人にそれぞれ高々1枚ずつコインを渡せる． 渡すコインは，その回の開始時に持っているものに限る． どのような座り方でも，5回の操作で全員のコインを5枚ずつにできることを示せ．
